How the protocol works

Rules users can inspect before they trade.

Positions are pooled in XLM. After a market closes, an authorized resolver publishes the outcome and the contract calculates each winning payout from the pool. The contract records fees by market and supports refunds when a market is cancelled.

Every Testnet transaction can be reviewed in Stellar Expert. Market resolution currently relies on authorized accounts; public feeds on this site are context, not automated oracles.

Why Stellar
Questions, answered

Know before you trade

Prediction markets reward correct outcomes, but every position carries risk.

What is a prediction market?

A market where participants take positions on verifiable future outcomes. Prices can reflect the crowd's current estimate of probability.

Can I place a position here today?

The configured Testnet market's published close date has passed, so it no longer accepts positions. The wallet and transaction integration demonstrates how a future open market will work. Testnet assets have no mainnet value.

Who resolves a market?

An authorized resolver submits the result after the close time. Public market data shown on this site does not trigger contract settlement automatically.

Why build on Stellar?

Stellar provides public, inspectable settlement and a direct path for users to hold and transfer assets with their own wallets.
